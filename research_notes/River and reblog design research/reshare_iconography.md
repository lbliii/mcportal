# Reshare iconography and interaction design (reblog / repost / retweet / boost)

Research date: 2026-10-02. Where a claim comes from reading source code (Bluesky social-app, Mastodon, Lucide, Phosphor, Tabler, Material Symbols), the link is to the repo file on its `main` branch as of this date. Shape descriptions were derived by reading the SVG path data directly, not from screenshots.

## 1. Platform reshare icons: construction, origin, revisions

### Takeaway
Nearly every platform (Twitter/X, Tumblr, Bluesky, Threads, Instagram 2025, Substack, Mastodon until its redesign) uses the same "two-arrow loop" glyph. It is two L- or U-shaped strokes, each ending in an arrowhead. The top one runs left-to-right and the bottom one right-to-left, so together they make a rectangular or rounded-rectangle loop with open diagonal corners. Brands set themselves apart through corner radius, stroke weight, gap placement, and the active-state treatment, not by changing the metaphor. Mastodon is the notable outlier: its in-progress 5.0 redesign (September 2026) swaps the rectangular loop for circular "arrows clockwise".

### Cited Findings

**Twitter / X**
- Retweeting started as a user convention ("RT @user") about two years before Twitter made it native. The first ReTweet is often credited to Eric Rice. Twitter began a limited rollout of a native retweet feature on 2009-11-05 and added a one-click retweet icon to the web interface, producing verbatim copies (so "fake" RTs were no longer possible). — [Anne Helmond, "A short history of retweets" (2013)](https://www.annehelmond.nl/2013/01/19/on-retweet-analysis-and-a-short-history-of-retweets/)
- The retweet active color has long been green. Around 2016 the shade reportedly moved from a forest green to a brighter minty green. — [Dubawa fact-check](https://dubawa.org/how-true-is-claim-twitter-changed-retweet-button-to-green-in-solidarity-with-june12thprotest/); [ICIR Nigeria](https://www.icirnigeria.org/twitter-did-not-change-like-and-retweet-button-colour-in-solidarity-with-june-12-protest/) (secondary sources, so treat the shade history as approximate)
- In 2015 Twitter replaced the star "favorite" with a heart "like". The like animation (heart, a bubble behind it, and particles; 28 frames in the original sprite) is credited to Vu Nguyen and has been widely re-implemented as "heart-burst" CSS sprite animations. — [Dribbble, Vu Nguyen](https://dribbble.com/shots/2416983-Twitter-Heart-Animation); [CSS-Tricks recreation](https://css-tricks.com/recreating-the-twitter-heart-animation/); [codedaily breakdown](https://www.codedaily.io/tutorials/How-to-Create-Twitter-Exploding-Hearts)
- X rebrand: the app was renamed X on 2023-07-28. New terms of service effective 2023-09-29 renamed "tweet" to "post" and "retweet" to "repost". The icon below each post is still the two-arrow loop. — [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/296104/20230908/x-formerly-twitter-replaces-tweet-post-retweets-become-reposts-new.htm); [The Apple Post](https://www.theapplepost.com/2023/07/28/63506/twitter-app-updated-now-called-x-with-new-app-icon/); [Pop Base on X](https://x.com/PopBase/status/1685320104085757952?lang=en)
- In October 2023 X floated removing the like and repost buttons from the main timeline. This was a tease and was not shipped broadly. — [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/news/2023/10/06/x-removal-action-buttons/)

**Tumblr**
- The reblog concept came from Jonah Peretti's "Reblog" project at Eyebeam's R&D program, an open-source tool for individually run blogs. Tumblr built reblogging into its network. — [Wikipedia: Reblogging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reblogging)
- The reblog glyph is the same two-arrow square loop (also published as `mdi-tumblr-reblog` in Material Design Icons community sets). — [shadcn.io mdi-tumblr-reblog](https://www.shadcn.io/icon/mdi-tumblr-reblog); [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/378911/tumblr-reblog)
- Active state: the reblog button turns from gray to green once you have reblogged a post from that account. Historically this was session-scoped: Tumblr did not persist "you reblogged this" the way it persists likes. — [Tumblr WIP staff answer (cyle)](https://www.tumblr.com/wip/702275578543439872/is-it-a-bug-or-the-intended-function-that-the); [user post, 2014](https://nothing67.tumblr.com/post/91630422877/i-swear-the-reblog-button-that-turns-green-makes)
- Motion: with the Alt+Reblog shortcut (instant reblog to the primary blog), the icon "turns green, explodes and then disappears," i.e., it spins and turns green. — [user post, ~Jan 2015](https://blazehedgehog.tumblr.com/post/107370638536/so-now-when-you-do-alt-reblog-the-reblog-symbol)
- Recent changes: Tumblr split likes, reblogs, and comments into separate counts, and the reblog button now opens more options, including "Add to queue". The 2023 desktop redesign moved navigation to a left column with text labels next to the icons. — [Tumblr Changes, 2023-06-02](https://changes.tumblr.com/post/719036393883598848/friday-june-2nd-2023); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/social-media/tumblr-changes-its-desktop-layout-now-resembles-twitter-but-without-all-the-drama); [Tumblr Support](https://support.tumblr.com/post/733358258142707712)

**Mastodon (boost)**
- "Boost" is reached through a circular-arrows icon. Older and third-party clients have used a rocket. — [fedi.tips](https://fedi.tips/how-do-i-do-likes-and-re-tweets-in-mastodon-and-what-are-bookmarks/)
- 2023-10-24, PR #27385 "Change icons in web UI": Mastodon replaced Font Awesome (Fork Awesome) with Material icons, "consistent with our Android app". The PR notes that Material allows "filled in for active, outline for inactive". — [mastodon/mastodon#27385](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/pull/27385)
- Legacy (Material) boost icon is `material-icons/400-24px/repeat.svg`. The boosted state uses a custom `repeat_active.svg`: the Material `repeat` loop plus a **solid 6×6 square filling the center of the loop** (`M9 9H15V15H9V9Z`). The fill change therefore signals state without relying on color. — [icons.tsx](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/mastodon/components/status/icons.tsx); [repeat_active.svg](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/svg-icons/repeat_active.svg)
- Material Symbols `repeat` (24px, outlined) construction: two right-angle "L" strokes with square (non-rounded) corners. The upper-left L rises from the left and turns right, ending in an arrowhead pointing right at top-right. The lower-right L rises at the right and runs left along the bottom, ending in an arrowhead pointing left at bottom-left. The arrowheads are chevrons and the strokes are thick (~80/960 units). — [Material Symbols repeat SVG](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/symbols/web/repeat/materialsymbolsoutlined/repeat_24px.svg)
- Active color: generic `.icon-button.active` uses `--color-text-brand` (Mastodon's blurple/blue), whereas favourite (star) uses `--color-text-favourite-highlight` (gold) and bookmark uses its own highlight. So **boosted = brand blue, not green**. However, the "someone boosted you" notification icon uses `--color-text-success` (green). — [components.scss](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/styles/mastodon/components.scss)
- Motion: the star (favourite) gets a `spring-rotate-in` keyframe animation (overshoots to -484.8°, settles at -360° over 1s), wrapped in a `.no-reduce-motion` class gate, so animation only runs when the user has not asked for reduced motion. No equivalent boost animation was found in current CSS. — [components.scss](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/styles/mastodon/components.scss)
- 2025: quote posts arrived. PR #35584 (2025-07-30) changed the quote-post design, and PR #35991 (2025-09-02) changed the quote icon variant. The legacy quote icon is Material `format_quote` (filled): two filled teardrop or comma-shaped quote marks. — [#35584](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/pull/35584); [#35991](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/pull/35991); [format_quote-fill.svg](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/material-icons/400-24px/format_quote-fill.svg)
- 2026-09-23, PR #40655 "Status redesign: Replace icons for status actions" (part of the 5.0 redesign, behind a flag): the redesign uses **Phosphor** icons. Boost becomes `ArrowsClockwiseIcon` (two circular arcs with arrowheads, i.e., a "refresh" shape). The new boosted icon (`boost_active.svg`) is two arcs plus a **filled disc in the middle**. Reply becomes `ChatCircle`, and like changes from **star to Heart** (filled when active). — [PR #40655](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/pull/40655); [boost_active.svg](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/svg-icons/boost_active.svg); [Mastodon 5.0 blog, Aug 2026](https://blog.joinmastodon.org/2026/08/5.0-laying-the-foundation/) (linked from the PR; blog not fetched)
- In the same `icons.tsx`, the quote mapping appears inverted relative to the others (redesign → Material `format_quote`, legacy → Phosphor `Quotes`). This may be a work-in-progress bug. — [icons.tsx](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/mastodon/components/status/icons.tsx)

**Bluesky (repost + quote)**
- The repost button uses `Repost_Stroke2_Corner3_Rounded`, a custom icon from Bluesky's own generated set. Construction from path data on a 24px grid with a 2px stroke: the top stroke starts at the left, rises (x=3, y≈11 → 9), and turns a large-radius (~4px) rounded corner into a horizontal bar running right to an arrowhead at top-right (x≈20.5, y≈6). The bottom stroke mirrors it: it descends at the right, turns a rounded corner, and runs left to an arrowhead at bottom-left (y≈18). Arrowheads have rounded tips. The set also includes Corner0 (square corners), Corner2 (medium radius), `RepostRepost` (two smaller staggered loops), and `RepostStrike` (loop with a diagonal slash for "quote posts disabled" or "repost disallowed"). — [Repost.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/icons/Repost.tsx)
- Active color: `activeColor={t.palette.positive_500}`, the "positive" (green) token. — [RepostButton.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/PostControls/RepostButton.tsx)
- Repost vs quote: tapping repost opens a Dialog titled "Repost or quote post". It has two large rows: Repost (repost icon, label changes to "Remove repost" when active) and "Quote post" (CloseQuote icon; shows "Quote posts disabled" in low-contrast text when the author has disabled quoting), plus a Cancel button. Long-press is wired separately (`onLongPress`), and haptics play on repost. — [RepostButton.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/PostControls/RepostButton.tsx)
- Accessible names include state and count: "Repost (# reposts)" versus "Undo repost (# reposts)", with translator comments. — [RepostButton.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/PostControls/RepostButton.tsx)
- In August 2024 Bluesky added quote-post detaching and controls to disable quoting. — [GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240829-bluesky-anti-toxicity-features/)

**Threads, Instagram, Substack, others**
- Threads: the repost icon is two arrows. Tapping it opens Repost / Quote, plus "Markup" (added ~2025). Quotes came to the web in 2023. — [Threads official post](https://www.threads.com/@threads/post/DFYVYVVRgFy?hl=en); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/threads-now-lets-you-quote-posts-on-the-web-083759052.html); [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/what-do-the-symbols-and-icons-mean-on-threads/)
- Instagram: a native repost button rolled out globally around 2025-08-06/07 for public feed posts and reels. It is a two-arrow loop placed next to like, comment, and share, with an optional note after reposting, and reposts appear in a dedicated profile tab. — [UK CCD news](https://ccd.uky.edu/news/instagram-adds-reposts-map-and-new-friends-tab-their-social-media-platform); [Minter.io](https://minter.io/blog/how-to-use-the-new-instagram-repost-feature/)
- Substack Notes "restack": a circle-arrow (two arrows forming a loop) icon with two options, "Restack" (as-is) or "Restack with a note". Readers can also highlight text in a post and restack the snippet. — [Substack support](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/14671398684308-What-is-a-restack-on-Substack-Notes); [On Substack: Notes](https://on.substack.com/p/notes)
- General "share" glyph lineage, which is distinct from reshare: Alex King designed the ShareThis icon (three connected dots) in 2006, and ShareThis acquired it a year later. Apple uses a box with an up arrow. Facebook and YouTube use an arrow that goes up and then curves right. — [Wikipedia: Share icon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_icon)

### Inferences
- The two-arrow loop has become the cross-platform convention for "put this on my feed". The 2025 Instagram adoption confirms that even Meta chose it over its own "send" paper-plane. "Share" (send elsewhere: up-arrow box, curved arrow, paper plane) is a different action with different glyphs, and the two should not be merged.
- Green for "reshared" is near-universal (Twitter/X, Tumblr, Bluesky). Mastodon's in-button blue is the outlier, though even Mastodon uses green for boost notifications.
- The dominant UX pattern for quote posts is a menu or sheet behind the reshare button (Bluesky, Threads, X, Substack, Mastodon), not a separate quote icon in the action bar.

### Gaps
- I could not find a primary source for who drew Twitter's 2009 retweet icon or its exact original geometry, nor official hex values for Twitter/X retweet green across eras (#19CF86, #17BF63, #00BA7C are widely cited but unverified here).
- I found no primary source dating Tumblr's reblog icon revisions (outline vs filled versions) or the exact current green hex. Whether reblogged state now persists across sessions was not confirmed.
- No reliable sources were found on the exact iconography of LinkedIn repost, Facebook share, Reddit crosspost, Pinterest save, Are.na "Connect", or Cohost share/repost. Searches returned only generic results. From memory (unverified): Reddit crosspost is a forked/branching arrow, Pinterest "Save" is a red text button, and Are.na "Connect" is a text button.
- Bluesky `positive_500` hex not confirmed (themes.ts grep returned nothing; the token likely lives in a palette file).

## 2. Icon libraries and the de facto "repost" glyph; confusion pitfalls

### Takeaway
The de facto repost glyph is the rectangular "repeat" loop (Material `repeat`, Lucide `repeat-2`, Tabler `repeat`, Phosphor `Repeat`, SF Symbols `arrow.2.squarepath`). Its main ambiguities are with the media "repeat/loop" control (often the very same glyph) and with circular "refresh/sync/cached" arrows. Mastodon's move to circular `ArrowsClockwise` pushes toward the refresh meaning.

### Cited Findings
- **Lucide `repeat-2`** is explicitly tagged `retweet`, `repost`, `share`, `repeat`, `loop` and categorized under arrows, social, and multimedia. Construction (24px, 2px stroke): left stroke goes up from (5,16) to (5,6) with an arrowhead pointing **up** at top-left (`m2 9 3-3 3 3`). The top bar runs from (11,6) to (17,6), turns a 2px radius corner, and goes down to (19,18), where an arrowhead points **down** at bottom-right. The bottom bar runs from (13,18) back to (7,18). So it is two U-ish paths with arrowheads on the **vertical** legs and gaps in the middle of the top and bottom edges. This is the Twitter-classic orientation. — [repeat-2.svg](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/repeat-2.svg); [repeat-2.json](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/repeat-2.json)
- **Lucide `repeat`** (tags `loop`, `arrows`; categories arrows, multimedia): arrowheads on the horizontal ends (top-right pointing right, bottom-left pointing left) with 4px rounded corners. This is the media-player orientation. — [repeat.svg](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/repeat.svg); [repeat.json](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/repeat.json)
- **Lucide `refresh-cw`** (tags rotate, reload, rerun, synchronize, circular, cycle): two 9-radius arcs with corner-bracket arrowheads. — [refresh-cw.svg](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/refresh-cw.svg)
- **Tabler `repeat`** is categorized **Media** (tags reuse, redo, replay, loop, flip, repeat): two strokes with 3px corners and horizontal arrowheads. — [tabler repeat.svg](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/icons/outline/repeat.svg)
- **Phosphor `Repeat`**: two large-radius (64/256) arcs that make a pill-like loop, with chevron arrowheads at top-right (pointing right) and bottom-left (pointing left). **Phosphor `ArrowsClockwise`**: two circular arcs, each ending in an L-bracket arrowhead at top-right and bottom-left. This is the refresh metaphor, now Mastodon's redesign boost. — [Phosphor repeat.svg](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core/blob/main/raw/regular/repeat.svg); [arrows-clockwise.svg](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core/blob/main/raw/regular/arrows-clockwise.svg)
- **Material Symbols**: `repeat` is the square-cornered rectangular loop described above. `cached` is two open circular arcs with arrowheads at the left and right, the standard refresh shape. `sync` is two vertical crescent arcs with corner-bracket arrowheads. `autorenew` is two circular arcs with arrowheads at top and bottom. All except `repeat` read as refresh or sync. — [repeat](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/symbols/web/repeat/materialsymbolsoutlined/repeat_24px.svg); [cached](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/symbols/web/cached/materialsymbolsoutlined/cached_24px.svg); [sync](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/symbols/web/sync/materialsymbolsoutlined/sync_24px.svg); [autorenew](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/symbols/web/autorenew/materialsymbolsoutlined/autorenew_24px.svg)
- **SF Symbols**: `arrow.2.squarepath` exists and is the conventional iOS repost glyph. One project bug shows that `arrow.2.squarepath.circle(.fill)` variants **do not exist** and render as empty glyphs, a pitfall when building a "reposted" filled state on iOS. — [meeshy issue #8939](https://github.com/isopen-io/meeshy/issues/8939); [Apple SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/)

### Inferences
- Because several libraries (Tabler, Lucide `repeat`) put this glyph under "media", placing it in a post action bar next to a heart and speech bubble is what makes it read as "repost". Context matters more than the glyph.
- Rectangular/square loops read as reshare, while circular arrows read as refresh, sync, or reload. For a reading app that also has refresh/sync actions (feed fetch), keep reshare rectangular and reserve circles for refresh.
- A filled-center active state (Mastodon's filled square or disc) is a good, color-independent "on" signal for a stroke-only loop that has no interior to fill.

### Gaps
- `arrow.triangle.2.circlepath` construction was not verified from Apple sources (it is generally the circular "sync" symbol).
- No Primer (GitHub) guidance on repost icons was found or checked.

## 3. Active-state conventions, motion, and accessibility

### Takeaway
Best practice combines a color change (green by convention), a shape or fill change (so color is not the only signal), a count update, optional brief motion gated by reduced-motion preferences, and an accessible name that either (a) stays fixed with `aria-pressed`, or (b) changes ("Repost" → "Undo repost") without `aria-pressed`, never both. Targets should be at least 24×24 CSS px (WCAG 2.2 AA), with 44×44 as the enhanced goal.

### Cited Findings
- MDN `aria-pressed`: adding it makes a toggle button. "Do not change the contents of the label on a toggle when the state changes". If you want the label to change, use a plain `<button>` and toggle the label instead of `aria-pressed`. — [MDN aria-pressed](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Reference/Attributes/aria-pressed)
- Bluesky follows the label-change approach ("Repost (n reposts)" / "Undo repost (n reposts)"), with the count included in the accessible name. — [RepostButton.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/PostControls/RepostButton.tsx)
- Icon-only buttons: treat the SVG as decorative and name the button with visually hidden text, `aria-labelledby`, or `aria-label` on the button. `aria-label` or `<title>` on the SVG fails in some screen reader combinations. — [Sara Soueidan, Accessible icon buttons](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/blog/accessible-icon-buttons/)
- WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 (AA): targets of at least 24×24 CSS px, or spacing such that 24px circles centered on each target don't overlap. SC 2.5.5 (AAA): 44×44. — [W3C Understanding 2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)
- Reduced motion: Mastodon gates its favourite spring animation behind a `.no-reduce-motion` class. — [components.scss](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/styles/mastodon/components.scss)
- Shape-plus-color precedent: Mastodon's boosted icon adds a filled center square (legacy) or disc (redesign), and liked/bookmarked switch to filled variants. — [icons.tsx](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/mastodon/components/status/icons.tsx); [PR #27385](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/pull/27385)
- Motion precedents: Twitter's 28-frame heart burst ([codedaily](https://www.codedaily.io/tutorials/How-to-Create-Twitter-Exploding-Hearts)), Tumblr's spin-and-green reblog ([user post](https://blazehedgehog.tumblr.com/post/107370638536/so-now-when-you-do-alt-reblog-the-reblog-symbol)), and Bluesky's haptic on repost ([RepostButton.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/PostControls/RepostButton.tsx)).

### Inferences
- WCAG 1.4.1 (Use of Color) means green alone cannot be the only "reposted" signal. Pair it with a fill or inner mark, a label change, or a count change. (I did not fetch the 1.4.1 text itself; this is the standard reading.)
- A reshare that opens a menu (repost / quote / queue) is arguably not a toggle. It is a menu button (`aria-haspopup`) whose state is better expressed through its label.

### Gaps
- I found no first-party documentation of Bluesky's repost animation (beyond haptics) or of current Tumblr/X animation specs.

## 4. Distinctive brand twists and icon recognizability research

### Takeaway
NN/g's research says only home, print, and search are near-universal, and icons need visible labels. A distinctive reshare icon therefore stays recognizable if it keeps the core topology (two opposing arrows forming a loop, placed in the post action row) and pairs it with a visible label or pulp-voiced verb. Styling (corner treatment, stroke weight, print texture, an inner motif) is where personality can go.

### Cited Findings
- NN/g "Icon Usability" (2014-07-27): only home, print, and magnifying-glass search are universally recognized. "A text label must be present alongside an icon to clarify its meaning," and labels must be visible, not hover-only. Their "5-second rule": if you can't think of an icon for a concept within 5 seconds, it probably can't communicate it. Their case example: a recognizable clock icon used for history got zero clicks, because recognizability is not the same as interpretability. — [NN/g Icon Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/icon-usability/); [NN/g video: interpretation vs recognizability](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/icon-interpretation-vs-recognizability/); [NN/g video: icons need text labels](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/icon-text-labels/)
- Bluesky shows a twist within the convention: a single custom set generated in variants by stroke weight and corner radius (`Stroke2_Corner0/2/3_Rounded`), with brand-specific semantic variants (`RepostRepost`, `RepostStrike`). — [Repost.tsx](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/src/components/icons/Repost.tsx)
- Mastodon shows a twist through state glyphs (a filled square or disc inside the loop) rather than color alone. — [repeat_active.svg](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/svg-icons/repeat_active.svg); [boost_active.svg](https://github.com/mastodon/mastodon/blob/main/app/javascript/svg-icons/boost_active.svg)
- Tumblr's 2023 desktop redesign added text labels to its icon navigation column, in line with NN/g's guidance. — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/social-media/tumblr-changes-its-desktop-layout-now-resembles-twitter-but-without-all-the-drama)
- Platform verbs as brand voice: "boost" (Mastodon), "restack" (Substack), "reblog" (Tumblr), "repost" (X, Bluesky, Threads, Instagram) all share essentially the same glyph, so the branding lives in the verb. — sources above ([fedi.tips](https://fedi.tips/how-do-i-do-likes-and-re-tweets-in-mastodon-and-what-are-bookmarks/), [Substack support](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/14671398684308-What-is-a-restack-on-Substack-Notes), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/296104/20230908/x-formerly-twitter-replaces-tweet-post-retweets-become-reposts-new.htm))

### Inferences (design guidance for MCPortal)
- Keep the topology: two opposing arrows forming a rectangular loop, square or slightly rounded, with a 2px stroke on a 24 grid to match the set. Avoid circular arcs so it doesn't read as refresh or sync.
- Add the twist inside the loop. The empty center of the loop is a natural place for the "portal" mark (for example a small portal/oval or the "line" mark), echoing Mastodon's filled-center active state. Inactive: outline loop with an empty or outline portal. Active: the portal fills (shape signal), plus the reblog color, plus the label "Reblogged" or "Undo reblog".
- Print-era treatment: square line caps or blunt arrowheads, slight ink-trap notches at the corners, or a misregistration offset used only for the active state (a second-color plate shifted 1px) suits the vintage pulp style without changing the silhouette. Test at 16px.
- Color: green is the learned convention. A pulp palette could use a printed-ink green (a duller, warmer green) to stay in brand while preserving the association. Don't rely on it alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
- Always pair the icon with a visible verb at least in the post detail and menu (NN/g). A pulp verb ("Reblog", or a flavored menu line such as "Send through the portal") can live in the menu, but keep "Reblog" as the primary accessible name for recognizability.
- Put quote/"reblog with note" in a menu or sheet behind the button (Bluesky/Threads pattern) rather than adding a second action-bar icon.
- Motion: a brief rotate or "ink stamp" on activation, gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`. Don't animate on undo.
