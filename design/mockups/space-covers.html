Space covers · mockup

One sample Space (@ana), drawn three ways. The cover art comes from the real src/ui/art.js engine, so these are covers a Space would actually get. Before the owner picks anything, the ink, motif and composition are all seeded from the handle. Change the ink or motif, or re-roll the composition, and all three directions update. View as Owner shows the edit marks, the print-shop bar and the owner-only stats. On the right of each direction is how a pasted Space link would look in Slack.

A · Paperback

A cover band across the top, the title set over it, a pinned "Now transmitting" post, and sources and people in a side column. Closest to the landing page.

Link preview

The preview is the same cover with a title plate on it, like a paperback spine label. It shows only what a visitor would see anyway: the handle, the Space title and art made from them.

B · Pulp magazine

The Space as a pulp magazine. The title is the masthead and the post count becomes "No. 42". The newest post is the cover story and the rest are "In this issue". The loudest of the three, with the most personality.

Link preview

The masthead does the branding. "Vol. II" counts the Space's years, which gives long-time users a quiet bit of status.

C · Mission patch

Compact and built for the chat card: a round patch with the cover art, the handle as a call sign, stamps, and tabs for posts, sources and travelers. The art could also serve as each person's avatar across MCPortal.

Link preview

The patch doubles as an avatar, so the same mark appears beside @ana in the river, the Lobby and the People portal. Your art becomes your face across MCPortal.
