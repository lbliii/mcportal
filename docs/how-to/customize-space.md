# Customize your Space

A Space is a publication of what you choose to share. Sign in and ask your agent to help claim a handle, then open **My space**. Your room, saved items, clips and private collections remain yours; sharing is a separate action.

## Choose a cover and format

Open the **print shop** on your Space. Choose an ink set, motif and one of three formats:

| Format | Shape |
|---|---|
| Paperback | A cover band with a side column |
| Magazine | A masthead with a cover story |
| Patch | A mission patch with section tabs |

Save your choices. **Re-roll** makes a new cover composition while keeping the selected ink and motif. Switching formats keeps your posts and profile fields. The same choices appear in your chat card and public web page.

Ask your agent to set your title, display name and bio, choose up to four transmitting topics, pin one of your own posts, or feature up to six listed fellow travelers. Approve the exact bio and topic wording before it is saved. You can hide individual earned stamps.

## Choose who can see and find you

A newly claimed Space is public by default. Uncheck **Public Space** when claiming your handle or in the print shop to keep it within signed-in MCPortal. Public Spaces have a page at `https://mcportal.lol/@handle` and an RSS feed at `https://mcportal.lol/@handle/feed`; self-hosted instances use their own domain.

Discovery listing is a separate choice, off by default. Turn it on if you want `find_people` to suggest you from the sources, topics and posts you have made visible.

For each share or reblog, choose **Public** or **Followers only** beside submission. Your explicit choice is remembered for future posts; when you have never chosen, new posts default to Public. Existing posts keep their audience. Public Space visibility still applies: a public post on a members-only Space stays off the web.

The web page and feed exclude followers-only posts. Quotes are limited to 400 characters; other clips require sign-in. Making the Space private withdraws the public page and feed, but cannot recall copies or feed-reader caches.

## Show sources and people

Automatic sections are currently on `main` under [Unreleased](../../CHANGELOG.md#unreleased). Open your own Space to preview eligible public room sources and people you follow. Enable each list independently only after checking the preview.

Pins, order and hidden entries curate these lists without subscribing, unsubscribing, following or unfollowing anyone. Previously featured recommendations keep their order. Private integrations, Shop follows, saved items, clips, docs portals and local or credential-bearing sources are excluded from automatic sources. Eligibility checks cannot recognize every secret URL, so review the actual candidates before enabling a list.

Automatic lists currently appear for signed-in Space visitors. Public web pages retain your explicitly published recommendations and authored fellow-traveler list.

For exact profile fields, see [Tools](../reference/tools.md#social). [Social](../explanation/social.md) explains discovery, reblog permissions and public-page behavior.
