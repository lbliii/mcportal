# Following stores

Store follows are currently on `main` under [Unreleased](../../CHANGELOG.md#unreleased); hosted and plugin installs need a release and deployment that includes them. For page changes, repository releases, calendars and artists, use [reading watches](watch-reading.md).

Shop follows supported public Shopify catalogues. Ask your agent to follow a store, or use **+ → Shopify store** in your room. Paste the store’s HTTPS URL, optionally enter a collection handle from `/collections/handle`, and choose **Sales only** if desired. Scan shows up to four products and the exact scope. Nothing is followed until you choose **Follow this store**.

Shop appears after confirmation. The first catalogue is your baseline. Later checks can show new arrivals, price drops, a sale starting, and restocks of saved product URLs. Variant IDs and currencies must match before prices are compared. A product created before the last check is not called a new arrival merely because it entered a bounded result. Price rises clear obsolete price-drop comparisons. Cards name the priced variant, currency, stock and observation time. Product links open at the store; **Save** keeps the link in Saved.

Opening Shop checks follows whose snapshot is at least a day old. **Refresh Shop** requests a fresh check, at most once per minute for each public store/collection query. Store controls show scope, pause state, last successful check, partial coverage and failures. Pause/resume or remove the follow there. Saved links survive removal. To change scope, preview the new scope and confirm it, then remove the old follow. An identical origin and scope is deduplicated; distinct collections may be followed separately. Removing the Shop portal preserves follows; `watch` confirmation restores the portal.

Only Shopify’s [tokenless Storefront API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest), version 2026-10, is supported in this delivery. Tokenless Products and Collections are documented; stores can deny access or automated traffic. MCPortal checks robots.txt using its identifiable fetch boundary, permits up to three safe public redirects for that policy, and sends no credentials or shopper identity. Catalogue POSTs do not follow redirects. A blocked policy, unsupported API or missing collection gives a clear error. There is no HTML, Atom, UCP or undocumented JSON fallback.

Each collection retrieves at most 100 newest products in ten pages of ten, with ten variants per product, 1 MB per response and ten seconds per request. A follow retains at most 200 observations and 30 findings; an account has at most 20 follows and 5 MB of watch data. The collector allows 30 public collection loads per minute per server process and two concurrent follows per room load. Shared public responses are cached for a day. Preview selections live in the handling process for ten minutes; a restart or a different replica requires a fresh preview, so multi-replica deployments need session affinity for confirmation. Failed fetches preserve the prior snapshot; missing products in partial or rotating results do not imply removal or sold-out stock. Unknown stock and partial variants are labeled. Findings are bounded recent comparisons, not a complete history or an unread inbox.

Follows and comparisons are private account data. Local installs use atomic files; hosted installs use PostgreSQL when configured. Linked clients read and change the hosted account, and an unreachable host fails clearly without writing a local substitute. The full export is version 3 and includes follows and snapshots; import is additive and validates watch data before writing it. Account deletion removes the watch record. Public Space features cannot expose Shop follows.

This release has no background checking, notifications, events, individual product/size watches, price thresholds, grouped drops or checkout. Review the first store-follow release before continuing [the shopping roadmap](../plans/shops.md).
